Installing a home EV charger provides a faster, safer, and more convenient way to charge your electric vehicle compared to using a standard power outlet. Before installation, it is important to ensure your electrical system can safely handle the additional load.

## Key Things to Consider

* **Electrical Supply Capacity:**

   Your switchboard and incoming power supply need to be assessed to confirm they can support an EV charger without overloading the system.
* **Maximum Demand Assessment:**

   A qualified electrician can calculate your property's electrical demand and determine the suitable charger size.
* **Charger Selection:**

   Choose a charger that suits your vehicle, charging requirements, available power supply, and future needs.
* **Dedicated Circuit Installation:**

   EV chargers require a dedicated circuit with appropriate protection devices installed at the switchboard.
* **Switchboard Upgrades:**

   Older switchboards may require upgrades to safely accommodate an EV charger installation.
* **Cable Route & Installation Location:**

   The best charger location depends on parking position, cable distance, access, and protection from the elements.
* **Safety & Compliance Testing:**

   Installation must be completed, tested, and certified by a licensed electrician to meet Australian electrical standards.
